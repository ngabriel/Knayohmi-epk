Which one should I choose?
Choose Starter if you’re happy to follow a simple guide and update your content yourself once everything is live. Choose Pro if you’d rather send me your content and receive a finished EPK ready to share.
What’s not included
Starter is designed to be self-editable using the guide provided. If you’d like me to add content, adjust styling, or make changes for you, that’s covered in Pro or charged as an update.

About

I’m Naomi (Knayohmi) — a South London DJ and audiovisual artist. I built these EPKs because I kept seeing talented artists lose bookings with messy links, missing info, or pages that don’t work on mobile. This setup keeps everything a promoter needs in one clean link: who you are, what you sound like, highlights, and how to book you.

The aim: easy for you, easy for promoters, and a page that feels professional even if you’re independent.
