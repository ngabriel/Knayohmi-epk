Knayohmi (Naomi) is a UK-based DJ and artist whose sets are a masterclass in rhythm-driven club culture. Drawing from a childhood spent watching Diana Ross and Tina Turner VHS tapes on repeat, her approach to performance is rooted in soul, and a deep understanding of the dancefloor.

After developing her instincts in the London underground since 2014, Knayohmi solidified her place in the scene as a core resident for Stickyjam Music and This Is My Culture. The latter—a queer-led activist collective—has seen her play legendary sets, including the George Michael tribute at Hampstead Heath, where she blends house-rooted sounds with a spirit of radical self-expression.

Beyond the decks, Naomi’s background in Computational Arts informs her vision for music as a multisensory experience. Her sets move fluidly between soulful house and percussive tech-house, designed to leave dancers feeling lighter, refreshed, and energized. Now focused on international stages and festival lineups, Knayohmi represents a generation of DJs who honor the heritage of dance music while pushing its community-led future forward.
